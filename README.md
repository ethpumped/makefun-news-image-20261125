Nothing off the books. The Open Ledger keeps the record of
Tuesday 25 November 2026 the way a public register should be
kept: open on the table, in dark red and smoke ivory, where
any reader can inspect any entry and trace any figure back to
the card it came from. Thirty-seven stories are entered.
Nine regions report. Every entry is dated, and the ones that
are not today's are labelled with the day they belong to.

The register opens with its largest entry. NATO is sending
early-warning jets to Poland, after Russian missiles landed
there during the attack on Ukraine; Chancellor Merz's promise
of complete support is entered in the same hand, on the same
line of the record, because a promise made in public belongs
in public. Beside it, the strait: Iran says three tankers
were hit by mines at Hormuz — entered under CLAIMED, the
register's mark for an entry the record cannot yet verify,
kept visible rather than quietly dropped. And Madrid's 5–1
comeback over PSV, entered with its scorers named.

The rest of the record, open to inspection. Seven killed in
Gaza, entered by name of role: a Hamas security chief, three
policemen, three siblings, one a child. Baghdad's order to
seize the Kurdish oilfields. A count of Russian war dead past
700,000 — the heaviest figure in the book, tagged to its card
like every other. Fifty Boeing jets ordered by Xiamen
Airlines. Twelve coral species restored to the Great Barrier
Reef's living register. Brussels' new sanctions. Storms
bearing down on Thanksgiving travel. Seven firms targeted in
Washington over chip diversion. And the PCE report, due this
day: its line is ruled and left open, because the figure had
not landed when the record closed, and an open line is what
honesty looks like in a ledger.

Four entries were struck before they could be entered, and
the striking is itself on the record: a Mercury mission story
dated 23 November, climate talks from earlier in the month, a
Liverpool result from an earlier round, and launch hype for
a game with no release today. Nvidia's entry stands under
MONDAY CLOSE — inspected, verified, and dated to Monday,
where it happened.

Inspection furniture: the eight heaviest figures run along
the top, each carrying its card number. The map is pinned by
card number. The audit column walks the day from opening to
closing. Region counts are entered only for stories actually
in the book.

How the register was made: `compose_open.py` sets the page
twice — an open layout with one tall entry beside a stacked
pair, and a banded layout with three entries abreast — and
the duel between the two settings is kept with the build
notes. The open layout stands: it gives the largest entry the
room its size demands. `main.py` delivers the finished page
to `/output/entry.png` exactly as entered.
